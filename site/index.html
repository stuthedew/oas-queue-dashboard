Cleared means done plus dropped, counted on the item's closed: date. New counts items by their added: date; items without one (the earliest, pre-field items) are carried in, not counted as new. Open is untriaged, ready, needs-decision or blocked. Lanes use each item's current touches: against docket.toml workflow_paths, the same test docket next uses; the total also includes crossing and unplaced items. Dates are as written in the item files, and the last day is partial.

      
Filed is how many times the issue has come up: the item itself plus each capture bin/docket new matched to it and a session let stand, read from recurrences:. A match a session later judged wrong is marked withdrawn in the file and counts for nothing here, and one capture recorded twice counts once.
